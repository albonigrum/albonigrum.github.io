Privacy policy

Boardgames Bot Drive is a personal tool for adding board game cards to a Notion library. The Google account owner authorizes the bot to upload photos to a folder selected through Google Picker.

The bot receives photos supplied through Telegram, uploads them to the selected Google Drive folder, and stores a link to each uploaded photo in the corresponding Notion game card. The folder is shared with anyone who has its link, so uploaded photos can be viewed by people with the link. The bot reads file names in that folder to choose the first available name for a new photo.

The bot stores the OAuth refresh token in its private runtime configuration so it can continue uploading without asking for consent each time. It does not use Google Drive data for advertising or sell it. It does not search other Drive folders.

The account owner can revoke access in Google Account settings and remove photos from Drive. For questions about this tool or its data use, contact the address shown on the Google consent screen.
